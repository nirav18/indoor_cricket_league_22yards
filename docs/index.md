# Indoor Cricket Basic Rules Introduction

## 1. The Game Format
- 6 players per side
- 24 overs per game, 12 per side
- Everyone bowls 2 overs (mandatory)
- Each Pair for 4 overs, even if a batter of the pair is dismissed

## 2. Batting / Scoring Runs
- Bat in pairs, face 4 overs as a partnership
- Non-striker stands at the running crease (11 yards from stumps)
- If out, continue to bat for the full 4 overs (do not leave the court)
- All dismissals: **-5 runs** from team total (any runs from that delivery are lost)
- To score: complete a run from batting crease to running crease (1 run), plus bonus runs for where the ball is hit:
  - Side nets before halfway: 1 run + 1 for running = **2**
  - Side nets after halfway: 2 runs + 1 for running = **3**
  - Side net then back net (bowler's end): **bonus run**
  - Boundary net/wall (floor): 4 runs + 1 for running = **5**
  - Boundary net/wall (full): 6 runs + 1 for running = **7**
  - Back net/wall (keeper's end): **0 runs**
- Runs can be scored by running at all times; hitting the ball to nets/walls is a bonus
- **3rd Ball Rule:** After two non-scoring deliveries, batters must run on the next (unless it's a wide/no-ball)
- Ball is dead if stuck/trapped in netting, equipment, or obstruction
- Ball is live except:
  1. Fall of wicket
  2. End of over
  3. Ball goes off court
  4. Umpire/player calls dead ball (e.g., injury)
- To swap bats, notify umpire before leaving crease

## 3. Fielding
- 6 players in the fielding team
- Must have 2 players in each half of the court before delivery (running crease = halfway)
- After ball is released, fielders may move freely

## 4. Bowling
- Everyone bowls 2 overs
- 1 over = 6 balls

## 5. Extras
- Leg side wide: must be inside painted guidelines at batter's end
- Offside: ball must be within the pitch
- Wide: ball starts on pitch but goes off before passing batter
- Can be run out/stumped off a wide
- **No balls:**
  1. Full toss/bouncer (passes batter at waist height or above without bouncing)
  2. Ball off the pitch (lands outside pitch area)
  3. Fielding no ball (more than 2 players in either half; warning before penalty)
- Cannot be out caught/stumped off a no ball, but can be run out
- Extras: **2 runs** to total, not rebowled (except last over)

## 6. Dismissals / Getting Out
- Caught
- Run out
- Bowled
- Stumped
- Hit wicket

## 7. Skins
Teams play for “skins” as well as wins to earn league points. A skin point is won by outscoring your comparative batting pair in each innings.

**Example:**

| Pair | Team 1 | Team 2 | Point Won By |
|------|--------|--------|--------------|
| 1st  | 20     | 18     | Team 1       |
| 2nd  | 15     | 18     | Team 2       |
| 3rd  | 30     | 32     | Team 2       |
| 4th  | 20     | 20     | Team 2 (by virtue of winning 3rd pair skin) |

If a skin is tied, the point carries over; next skin winner gets two points. If final skin is tied, the team who won the third skin gets two points.

## 8. League Points
- **Win:** 3 Points
- **Tie:** 1 Point
- **Loss:** 0 Point
- **Skins:** 4 Skin Points per match

If teams are tied at season end, order is:
1. Points
2. Skins won
3. Run difference (scored minus conceded)
4. Runs scored

## 9. Match Duration & Timing
- Matches are time-based, not strictly ball-based
- Match clock runs continuously
- Delays do not stop the clock unless directed by officials

## 10. General & Conduct
- Umpire decisions are final
- Fair play and sportsmanship are mandatory
- League may clarify/amend rules as required

## 11. Player Substitutions
- Each team nominates 6 players before match start
- Substitutions only for injury/medical reasons (must be umpire-approved)
- Substitute may field only (not bat, bowl, or keep) except for like-for-like injury replacement
- No tactical/strategic substitutions after match begins
- Substituted player may not re-enter unless approved by officials

## 12. Lateness, Attendance & Penalties
- Matches start at scheduled time with running clock
- If late:
  - Up to 5 min: official warning
  - 10 min: 10-run penalty per late player (not cumulative)
- Minimum 5 players needed to start; less than 5 after 15 min may result in forfeiture

## The Spirit of Cricket

### Players
- Treat opponents with courtesy and respect; applaud good play from both teams
- Encourage bowlers within reasonable bounds (no excessive shouting/clapping)
- Only fielders in a good position should appeal
- Do not show dissent or question umpire’s decision
- Never make offensive comments/gestures to opponents
- Celebrate successes, but not excessively
- Thank umpires and scorers after the game (captains especially)

### Managers (IYKYK Whos Manager?!?!)
- Set a good example; avoid “win at all costs” attitude
- Do not shout at players on the field

### Spectators
- Support your team without interfering
- Support officials (umpires, scorers, managers, coaches)
- Do not instruct players (manager’s role)
- Do not make derogatory comments about umpires or opponents
